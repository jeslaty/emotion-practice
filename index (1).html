9年級 Unit 2 情緒動詞／形容詞

Emotion Verbs & Adjectives Practice

be interested in
對……感興趣
be excited about
對……感到興奮
be bored with
對……感到厭煩
be surprised at
對……感到驚訝
先看整個句型，不要只看字尾：
① A interests / excites / bores / surprises B → 情緒動詞
② B is interested in / excited about / bored with / surprised at A → -ed 形容詞
③ A is interesting / exciting / boring / surprising → -ing 形容詞

特別注意： surprised 也可以是 surprise 的過去式。
The test grades surprised Lisa. → 過去式動詞
